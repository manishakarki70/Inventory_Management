# 📦 Inventory Management Dashboard – Power BI Project

## 🎯 Objective  
Designed a comprehensive **Inventory Management Dashboard** using Power BI to help warehouse and supply chain teams:
- Monitor stock levels
- Analyze supplier performance
- Identify restocking needs across global regions  

---

## 🔍 Business Problem  
Inefficient inventory management—whether overstocking or stockouts—can lead to increased warehousing costs, delayed fulfillment, and supplier issues.  

This dashboard was built to solve these challenges by providing:
- ✅ A real-time view of stock availability  
- 📦 Performance assessment of suppliers  
- 🌍 Geographic insights on inventory distribution  
- 🚨 Rapid identification of restocking needs  

---

## 🧠 Key Insights Delivered
- **Supplier Efficiency:** Identified top-performing and delayed suppliers based on average delivery time vs stock levels.  
- **Stockout Trends:** Highlighted inventory shortages by category, country, and time to help prioritize restocking.  
- **Reorder Planning:** Tracked reorder frequency and cost over time for procurement optimization.  
- **Risk Alerts:** Flagged products with high restock needs across global regions to support fast decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Features  

### 1️⃣ Inventory Overview
- **KPIs:** Total Quantity, Stock Value, % Out-of-stock, Previous Year, YoY Change  
- **Visuals:**  
  - Multi-category stock breakdown  
  - Product Distribution  
  - Country-level stock table with conditional formatting  
  - Interactive category analysis by stock Value

---

### 2️⃣ Supplier Performance
- **KPIs:** Total Reorders, Average Unit Price, Total Reorder Cost  
- **Visuals:**  
  - Monthly reorder trend (bar chart)  
  - Reorder cost by product category  
  - Supplier-wise stock & delivery (bubble chart: Avg Delivery Time vs Stock Quantity)  

---

### 3️⃣ Geographic Insights
- **KPIs:** Total Warehouses, Total Products, Categories
- **Visuals:**  
  - Table of products needing restock (with alert flags)  
  - Stock distribution by country  
  - Stock breakdown by country and level  
  - Explore stock across regions  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used
- **Power BI Desktop:** For report creation and data modeling  
- **DAX Measures:** KPIs, YoY%, % in-stock, reorder cost trends   
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and supplier/warehouse standardization  
- **UX/UI Design:** Custom themes, iconography, responsive layouts, and conditional formatting  

---

## 📈 Simulated Business Impact
- ⏱ **30% faster** identification of stockouts through visual alerts  
- 🤝 **Improved supplier negotiations** via performance insights  
- 📉 **Smarter procurement planning** with reorder cost trends and YoY analytics
